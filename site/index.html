Open Paint Format

An open, implementation-independent format for portable miniature-painting data: recipes, palettes, paint inventories, projects and techniques.

The specification, schemas, conformance corpus and reference validator live at github.com/openpaintformat/standard. Version 1 is a release candidate; it is not frozen yet.
